# Business Requirements

## Business Objective

To develop an executive Power BI solution that enables HR and business leaders to monitor workforce composition, identify elevated attrition and investigate potential retention drivers.

## Primary Stakeholders

- HR leadership
- HR business partners
- Workforce Planning teams
- Department Managers
- Executive leadership

## Functional Requirements

1. To display workforce size and active employee count.
2. To calculate overall attrition count and rate.
3. To compare attrition across departments and job roles.
4. To show attrition rate and exit volume separately.
5. To analyse attrition by gender, age, income and tenure.
6. To compare overtime attrition with the company benchmark.
7. To analyse job level, satisfaction and work-life balance.
8. To review promotion-gap risk and employee exposure.
9. To allow filtering by department and gender.
10. To provide navigation across analytical pages.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Consistent KPI definitions.
- Fast and intuitive filtering.
- Readable dashboard layout.
- Clear business labels.
- Correct sort order for categorical bands.
- Controlled access to sensitive data.
- Documented refresh and ownership responsibilities.

## Success Criteria

- Total employees equal 1,470.
- Attrition count equals 237.
- Attrition rate equals 16.12%.
- All pages respond correctly to slicers.
- Benchmark lines remain consistent under the intended filter context.
- Stakeholders can distinguish attrition rate from attrition volume.
